Roman numerals stay the same in every key: uppercase is a major chord, lowercase is minor, ° is diminished. Learn a progression as numbers once and you can play it anywhere.

Borrowed means a chord comes from outside the key, like the flat VII in rock or the major V in a minor key. It's what gives those progressions their extra pull.

The box is one hand position, about six frets wide. Switching to the 5th string root shows the neighboring box, which is a quick way to connect positions up the neck. Esc stops playback.
